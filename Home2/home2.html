Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know
about composting with us.

Find quick answers to common questions about our composting and organic waste management services.

What type of waste can I give to CompostCare?

We primarily handle organic and compostable waste such as fruit and vegetable scraps, food leftovers, coffee grounds, tea waste, and other suitable kitchen waste.

How does the waste collection process work?

We collect your organic waste from your selected location and take it through the appropriate sorting and composting process.

Can apartments and communities use CompostCare?

Yes. Our solutions can be adapted for individual homes, apartments, offices, and larger residential communities based on their organic waste requirements.

What happens to the collected organic waste?

The collected waste is carefully sorted and processed through natural composting methods, transforming suitable organic material into nutrient-rich compost.

How long does composting usually take?

Composting time can vary depending on the type and quantity of organic waste, moisture, temperature, and the composting method used.

Do I need to separate my waste before collection?

Separating organic waste from recyclable and non-compostable waste helps keep the collection process cleaner and makes composting more effective.
