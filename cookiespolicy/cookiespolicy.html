INTRODUCTION

Introduction

CompostCare uses cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, remember certain preferences, understand website usage, and improve the overall experience of our visitors and subscribers.

This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, how CompostCare may use them, and the choices available to you when you visit our website.

01

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your device when you visit a website. They can help websites remember information about your visit and provide certain functions.

Similar technologies may also be used to collect information about how a website is accessed and used.

02

How We Use Cookies

CompostCare may use cookies and similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • Supporting essential website functionality.
  • Remembering selected preferences.
  • Supporting subscriber account functionality.
  • Understanding how visitors use our website.
  • Improving website performance and user experience.
03

Types of Cookies We Use

The cookies used by a website can serve different purposes. Depending on how the CompostCare website and services are configured, cookies may include essential, preference, and analytics-related cookies.

The specific cookies used may change as website functionality and services are updated.

04

Essential Cookies

Essential cookies may be required for certain website functions to operate correctly. They can support features such as navigation, security, session management, and access to necessary account functions.

Because these cookies may support essential functionality, restricting them may affect parts of the website.

05

Preference Cookies

Preference cookies may help remember choices you make while using the website, such as certain interface preferences or settings.

These cookies can help provide a more consistent experience when you return to the website.

06

Analytics and Performance

Where analytics technologies are used, they may help us understand how visitors interact with the website, which pages are viewed, and how the website performs.

This information can help CompostCare identify areas where the website or services may be improved.

07

Third-Party Technologies

Some website features or services may be provided through third-party technologies. These providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies and practices.

CompostCare does not control the cookie practices of third-party services. You should review the relevant third-party privacy or cookie policies when applicable.

08

Managing Cookie Preferences

You can manage or restrict cookies through your browser settings. Your browser may allow you to block cookies, delete existing cookies, or receive notifications before cookies are stored.

If you disable certain cookies, some website features may not function as intended.

09

Changes to This Policy

CompostCare may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, technologies, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will include a revised "Last Updated" date.
