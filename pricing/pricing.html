CLEAR ANSWERS. CONFIDENT CHOICES.

Everything You Need To Know Before Choosing A Plan

Find quick answers about our plans, services and scheduling to choose the right solution with confidence.

What is included in each pricing plan?

Each plan includes services designed around your needs, from home composting support to community and business waste-management solutions.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can change your plan as your composting and waste-management requirements grow or change.

How does waste pickup scheduling work?

Pickup services can be scheduled according to your selected plan and preferred service requirements.

Are composting kits included in the monthly price?

The Home Plan includes a composting kit along with setup guidance to help you get started.

Can businesses and apartment communities customize their service?

Yes. Community and business requirements can vary, so service arrangements can be adapted to the scale and needs of each location.

Is there any additional charge for pickup or setup?

Any additional service requirements or charges can be confirmed based on the selected plan and requested service.
