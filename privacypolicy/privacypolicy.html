INTRODUCTION

Introduction

CompostCare respects your privacy and is committed to handling your personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you when you use our website and services.

This policy also explains how information may be handled when you use your CompostCare subscriber account and dashboard.

01

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide when you create an account, subscribe to a plan, schedule a pickup, contact us, or use CompostCare services.

Personal Information

This may include your name, email address, phone number, and other contact information that you choose to provide.

Account Information

When you use the subscriber portal, we may process information associated with your account, subscription, pickup schedule, delivery status, and service activity.

Payment Information

We may maintain records related to subscriptions and payments. Payment details may be processed by the payment service used to complete a transaction.

02

How We Use Your Information

CompostCare may use collected information to provide, operate, maintain, and improve our services.

  • Manage your CompostCare account.
  • Process and manage subscriptions.
  • Schedule and manage waste pickups.
  • Provide compost delivery updates.
  • Maintain payment and transaction records.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Provide relevant sustainability information.
  • Improve our website and services.
03

Cookies and Technologies

CompostCare may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, remember preferences, understand how visitors use the website, and improve the overall user experience.

You may be able to control or restrict cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

04

Information Sharing

CompostCare may share information when necessary to provide requested services, process transactions, operate website features, provide technical services, comply with legal obligations, or protect the rights and security of CompostCare and its users.

We do not use your personal information for purposes unrelated to our services without an appropriate basis for doing so.

05

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of storing or transmitting information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

06

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain appropriate business and transaction records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and enforce applicable agreements.

07

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the ability to request access, correction, or deletion of certain information.

You may contact CompostCare if you have questions about your personal information or wish to make a privacy-related request.

08

Children's Privacy

CompostCare services are not intended for children who are not permitted to use such services under applicable law. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

09

Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, practices, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated policy will include a revised "Last Updated" date.

10
